# Proposal: 24/7 booking assistant for BrightSmile Dental

Prepared for Hoa Tran, Operations Manager, and Dr. Minh Le, Director · Northbeam Studio · 20 Nov 2026 · Valid for 30 days

## Summary

Your front desk spends the morning answering the same questions, and messages that arrive after 7pm or on Sundays turn into bookings at other clinics. We will build a bilingual (Vietnamese and English) assistant for your website and Facebook Messenger that answers common questions, books, moves and cancels appointments directly in your dentists' Google Calendars, sends a reminder the day before, and hands patients to your team whenever a human is needed. We recommend the **Recommended option at $10,970**, live at all 4 clinics by **8 Jan 2027** - a week before the pre-Tet rush - if we start on 1 Dec 2026.

## What you told us

- The front desk answers the same questions all morning: opening hours, cleaning and whitening prices, accepted insurance, booking changes.
- Messages after 7pm and on Sundays are missed, and those patients book elsewhere.
- About 30% of patients are expats, so the assistant must work in Vietnamese and English.
- 4 clinics and 11 dentists, each with their own Google Calendar; bookings are entered by hand today.
- Medical questions and unhappy patients must go to a person, and the front desk must see the conversation.
- You want to know how many bookings come from the assistant each month.
- It must be live before Tet (6 Feb 2027); budget around $6,000-9,000, with room to stretch for the right solution.

## Our approach

We build the assistant on a proven AI language model with strict guardrails: it answers only from the price list, opening hours and FAQ you approve, never gives medical advice, and books by reading each dentist's live calendar - so it can never double-book a slot your team filled by hand. You keep working in Google Calendar exactly as today.

The phases below describe the Recommended option; the Investment section shows what each option includes.

### Phase 1 - Discovery and conversation design (weeks 1-2)

**You get:** an approved script of everything the assistant will say, and written booking rules.

- 2-hour workshop with your front desk leads to capture questions, services, slot lengths and booking rules per clinic.
- About 25 questions and the booking, rescheduling and cancelling flows written in Vietnamese, then in English.
- Handover rules: which topics always go to a person, and who is alerted at each clinic.

### Phase 2 - Build (weeks 3-4)

**You get:** a working assistant on a test copy of your website and Messenger page.

- Chat widget on your WordPress website and connection to your Facebook page's Messenger.
- Booking, rescheduling and cancelling across all 11 dentist calendars, with the clinic and dentist the patient prefers.
- Instant alert to the clinic's front desk on handover, with the full conversation attached.
- Day-before SMS reminder for every appointment booked or confirmed through the assistant.
- Monthly report emailed to Dr. Minh: bookings, reschedules and cancellations made by the assistant, per clinic.

### Phase 3 - Test, train and launch (weeks 5-6)

**You get:** a live assistant at all 4 clinics and a team that knows how to work with it.

- Test scripts and 3 days of testing with 2 of your staff; fixes from their feedback.
- 2 training sessions (60 minutes each) and a short how-to guide in Vietnamese.
- Soft launch at District 1 on 4 Jan, then all clinics by 8 Jan 2027.
- 30 days of bug fixes after launch, covering the whole pre-Tet period.

## Out of scope

- **Zalo Official Account chat and Zalo reminders** - included in the Complete option.
- **Deposits for whitening appointments** - included in the Complete option.
- **Admin dashboard** with a live handover queue - the Recommended option uses alerts and a monthly email report instead; the dashboard is in the Complete option.
- **Integration with dental practice-management software** - not mentioned in your brief; we can scope it later if you use one.
- **Writing medical content** - the assistant refers medical questions to your team.
- **Changes to your website design** beyond adding the chat widget.

## Assumptions

- Your WordPress site lets us add a chat widget script (we need an admin login or your web developer's help for 30 minutes).
- All 4 clinics share one Facebook page. If each clinic has its own page, add 0.5 days per extra page.
- All 11 dentist calendars are in Google accounts you control, and staff keep using them as the single source of truth.
- Each service has a fixed appointment length we can agree on in the workshop.
- The price list, insurer list and opening hours change rarely; your team can update them in a shared document we connect to the assistant.
- Script approvals arrive within 2 business days of each review request.
- Patient data is limited to name, phone number, preferred clinic and appointment; conversations are kept for 90 days unless you choose otherwise.

## Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| SMS sender name registration takes longer than 4 weeks | We start in week 1. Until it is approved, reminders go by email and Messenger, and we switch to SMS without further work. |
| Staff edit calendars in ways the assistant does not expect | Booking rules agreed in the workshop; the assistant reads live availability before every booking and never overwrites existing events. |
| Patients ask medical questions | The assistant does not answer them; it states this and hands over to the clinic, with the conversation attached. |
| Approval or access delays push launch into the pre-Tet rush | Content freeze on 18 Dec; fallback plan to launch at 2 clinics first. |
| Patient data protection | Minimal data collected, encrypted storage, 90-day retention by default, and setup aligned with Vietnam's personal data protection rules. |
