**From:** Hoa Tran, Operations Manager, BrightSmile Dental
**Subject:** Chatbot for appointment booking - can you quote?

Hi,

We run 4 dental clinics in Ho Chi Minh City (District 1, District 3, District 7 and Thu Duc). Our front desk spends most of the morning answering the same questions on Zalo, Facebook Messenger and the website chat: opening hours, prices for cleaning and whitening, whether we accept a certain insurance, and booking or moving appointments. We miss a lot of messages after 7pm and on Sundays, and patients book with competitors instead.

What we want:

- A chatbot on our website and Facebook Messenger that answers common questions in **Vietnamese and English** (about 30% of our patients are expats).
- It should **book, reschedule and cancel appointments**. Each clinic has its own Google Calendar per dentist (11 dentists in total). Today the front desk books manually into these calendars.
- Send a reminder the day before the appointment. SMS would be nice; Zalo is what most patients use.
- Hand over to a human when the patient asks something medical or is unhappy. The front desk should see the conversation.
- Some kind of report: how many bookings came from the bot each month.

Nice to have: Zalo Official Account integration (we have an OA already), and collecting a deposit for whitening appointments.

We want this live before Tet (the clinics are very busy in the 3 weeks before Tet, which starts 6 Feb 2027). Our budget is around $6,000-9,000 but we can stretch for the right solution. Our website is on WordPress. We are not technical, so we need training and someone to call if it breaks.

Can you send a proposal by next week? Our director, Dr. Minh Le, makes the final decision.

Hoa
