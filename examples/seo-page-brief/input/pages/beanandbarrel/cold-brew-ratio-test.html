Bean & Barrel

We tested 5 cold brew ratios side by side

Every cold brew guide gives a different ratio, so we tested five of them. Same beans (a medium-roast Colombian), same coarse grind, same filtered water, 16 hours at room temperature, then diluted to the same drinking strength where needed. Four of us tasted them blind.

The setup

We used 1 litre of water for each jar and changed only the amount of coffee: 250 g for 1:4, 200 g for 1:5, 125 g for 1:8, 100 g for 1:10 and 83 g for 1:12. Concentrates were diluted with an equal amount of water before tasting.

The results

What we recommend

If you drink your cold brew with milk, brew a 1:5 concentrate. If you drink it black over ice, brew 1:8 and skip the dilution step. Nobody liked 1:4 enough to justify using twice as much coffee.

Cost per glass

At $16 per 500 g bag, a 1:5 concentrate costs about $0.80 per 250 ml glass once diluted, and a 1:8 ready-to-drink batch costs about $1.00 per glass. Concentrate stretches your beans further when you dilute it with milk.
