Looking for the perfect cold brew ratio? Here is our quick cheat sheet.

Ratios

  • Concentrate: 1 cup coffee to 4 cups water
  • Regular: 1 cup coffee to 8 cups water

Steep time

12 to 24 hours. Strain and enjoy.

Advertisement

Tips

  • Use coarse coffee.
  • Use filtered water.
  • Store in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.
