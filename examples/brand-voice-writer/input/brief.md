# Brief: launch of "Roaster's Choice"

> Fictional sample brief.

**Prompt used:** "Write the launch posts for Roaster's Choice in our voice (voice-guide.md): a LinkedIn post from Mai, an Instagram caption, and the email to our list. Check them before you send them to me."

**What it is:** a new subscription option. Instead of choosing a coffee, subscribers get whatever single origin the roasters are most excited about that week. Each bag comes with a card explaining why it was picked and a suggested recipe.

**Facts we can use:**

- Price: $19 per 250 g bag, delivery included. Every 2 or 4 weeks.
- Pause, skip or cancel any time from the account page.
- Launches Monday 7 December 2026. First shipment Wednesday 9 December.
- Only 150 subscriptions in the first month, because green coffee for this is bought in small lots.
- First coffee: a washed Ethiopian from Guji that tastes like peach and black tea.
- Existing subscribers can switch from their account page; no new sign-up needed.
- Link: lumencoffee.example/roasters-choice

**Audience:** existing email list (about 2,400 people, many already subscribers) and social followers.

**Goal:** fill the 150 spots in the first week. CTA: start or switch to Roaster's Choice.

**Avoid:** sounding like a "mystery box" gimmick. The point is trust in the roasters' taste.
