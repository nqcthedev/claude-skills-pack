# Lumen Coffee Co. voice guide

> Fictional brand used for examples. Built with `skills/brand-voice-writer/assets/voice-guide-template.md`.

## Who we are, in one line

A two-person roastery that roasts small batches every Monday and ships within 48 hours, for people who care how their coffee tastes but don't want a lecture about it.

## Audience

Home brewers aged 25-45, mostly in cities, who own a grinder and a pour-over or espresso machine. They are tired of "artisanal" packaging, vague tasting notes and coffee that was roasted months ago.

## Personality

| We are | We are not | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| Warm | Cute or gushing | "Good morning. Your beans left the roaster four hours ago." |
| Precise | Nerdy or preachy | "Roasted Monday. At your door by Wednesday." |
| Plain-spoken | Hype-driven | "It tastes like milk chocolate and hazelnut. That's it, no fireworks." |

## How we write

- Person: "we" for the roastery, "you" for the reader. The founders sign emails as "Mai & Tuan".
- Sentences: short, mostly under 20 words. Contractions are fine.
- Capitalisation: sentence case for headlines. Coffee names in Title Case (Yirgacheffe Washed).
- Numbers and money: numerals, "$18", "250 g", "48 hours".
- Emoji: at most 1 per piece, never in emails. Exclamation marks: at most 1 per piece.
- Hashtags: at most 3, only on Instagram and LinkedIn, lowercase (#specialtycoffee).
- Humour: dry and occasional, never at the customer's expense.

## Vocabulary

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| fresh, roasted on (date) | artisanal, craft, curated |
| tastes like | flavour profile, notes of (in headlines) |
| subscription | membership, club |
| we pick, we choose | handpicked, hand-selected |
| good, really good | amazing, incredible, game-changer |

## Claims we can and cannot make

- Yes: "roasted every Monday", "shipped within 48 hours of roasting", "free delivery on subscriptions", "pause or cancel any time".
- No: "best coffee in Vietnam", "ethically sourced" (we have not certified it), anything about health benefits.

## Channel notes

- LinkedIn: from the founders' point of view; behind-the-scenes and decisions. 120-200 words.
- Instagram: sensory, short, one image idea per post.
- Email: one idea per email, signed "Mai & Tuan", plain-text feel, one link.

## Examples

**On voice:** "We roasted 42 bags of Cerrado this morning. 38 are already sold. If you want one of the last four, they ship tomorrow." - *why it works:* concrete numbers, gentle urgency without hype.

**Off voice:** "Indulge in our artisanal, hand-selected beans for an amazing coffee experience!" - *what's wrong:* three banned words, vague, an exclamation mark doing the work a fact should do.
