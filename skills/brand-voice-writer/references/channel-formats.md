# Channel formats

Default structures and limits. A voice guide's own channel notes override these. The `--channel` names below are the ones `scripts/check_draft.py` understands; limits are words unless noted.

| Channel (`--channel`) | Length | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| `linkedin` | 80-220 words (checker max 250) | Hook line (under 15 words, stands alone before "see more") → 2-4 short paragraphs → one CTA → up to 3 hashtags at the end. |
| `x` | max 280 characters | One idea. Front-load the point. Link or CTA at the end. Max 1-2 hashtags, or none. |
| `instagram` | 40-150 words (checker max 200) | First line is the hook (shown in the feed) → story/detail → CTA ("link in bio", "comment...") → hashtags on a separate final line. |
| `facebook` | 40-120 words (checker max 150) | Conversational, one question or CTA. |
| `email` | 120-300 words body (checker max 350) | `Subject:` line (under 50 characters) and `Preview:` line (under 90 characters) at the top → greeting → benefit in the first two sentences → details as short paragraphs or 3-5 bullets → one button-style CTA line → sign-off. |
| `newsletter` | 250-600 words (checker max 700) | Subject + preview, then 2-4 sections with sentence-case subheadings, one primary CTA. |
| `product` | 60-160 words (checker max 200) | Benefit headline → 2-3 sentence description → 3-5 bullet specs → CTA. |
| `ad` | headline max 40 characters, body max 125 characters | Problem or desire → offer → CTA. Write 3 variants. |
| `generic` | no length limit | Use when nothing above fits. |

## Writing tips that hold across channels

- The first 7-10 words decide whether the rest is read; spend the most effort there.
- Prefer verbs to adjectives. "Roasted Monday, at your door Wednesday" beats "incredibly fresh coffee".
- Cut the first sentence of a draft; it is usually throat-clearing.
- Read it aloud. If you run out of breath, split the sentence.
